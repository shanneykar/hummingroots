How this works

Six things, so nothing here is a surprise.

  • No guarantees. Not here, not anywhere. Anyone selling you a guaranteed outcome is selling certainty, which this practice has never had.
  • A reading comes first. Nothing custom is taken on without one, and it frequently ends with me telling you no working is needed.
  • Materials and labor are priced separately. You are paying for roots that get consumed and hours that get spent. Nothing else.
  • Paid before it begins. Candles get dressed and materials get bought up front. Large work can be half now, half on the agreed date.
  • What hasn’t been spent comes back. Once altar work has started it can’t be returned — but before that, change your mind and say so.
  • Your name never leaves this practice. Not as an example, not as a story, not as content. I ask you to keep an active working quiet too — and if the silence slips, tell me. It is a human thing, not a betrayal.
