What I read

Five kinds of reading

Every one starts from your birth chart. Which reading you want depends on whether you're asking about a life, a match, a date, or a single question.

Birth chart reading

A full reading of the natal chart — temperament, work, relationships, family, health — and where you currently sit in the sequence of planetary periods. The right place to start if you've never had a chart read.

Compatibility reading

Two charts read side by side, for couples deciding whether to marry or for families weighing a match. Covers the traditional compatibility points, what supports the union, and where friction is likely.

Auspicious timing

Choosing a date and hour for something that matters — a wedding, a move, signing a business, beginning a course of study. You give the window and the event; I find the openings inside it.

Single-question reading

One question, read from the moment you ask it rather than from your birth chart. Useful when a decision is in front of you now and you want a direct answer rather than a full life reading.

Remedies

Practical measures matched to what the chart shows — mantra, charity, fasting days, gemstones where they're genuinely indicated. Given as something you can actually keep up, not a list you'll abandon in a week.
